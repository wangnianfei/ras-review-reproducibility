## Figure 11 | Global distribution of RAS-related oncology trials registered on ClinicalTrials.gov

**a**, Country distribution of the 1,133 interventional phase I–III oncology trials that reference *RAS* as a target or eligibility biomarker: shading, trials with at least one registered site; unit, trial–country pairs (1,054 trials with location data, 3,206 pairs, 72 countries); leader lines, the eleven busiest countries; Hong Kong (37 trials) merged into China for the map only. **b**, The 25 countries with most trials, stacked by highest phase; totals at bar ends. **c**, Cumulative trials by year first posted for the six leading countries (2026 truncated at 20 October). **d**, Portfolio of the 15 leading countries by relation to *RAS*: RAS-targeted, agent acts on RAS; RAS-regulator-targeted, acts on SOS1, SHP2 or farnesylation; RAS-biomarker-selected, *RAS* genotype governs eligibility or stratification; RAS-mentioned, mentioned only. ClinicalTrials.gov only (REST API v2, data cut 20 October 2026); studies registered solely on ChiCTR or with the Chinese regulator (e.g. the sosimerasib phase 2 monotherapy study, ChiCTR2200059986) are absent, so China's share is understated.

## 图11 ｜ ClinicalTrials.gov 登记的 RAS 相关恶性肿瘤临床试验的全球分布

**a**，1 133 项以 *RAS* 为靶点或入排生物标志物的 I–III 期干预性肿瘤试验的国家分布：颜色深浅，设有登记中心的试验数；计数单位，试验—国家对（有地点信息的试验 1 054 项，3 206 对，72 个国家/地区）；引线，试验数前 11 位国家；香港（37 项）仅制图时并入中国。**b**，试验数前 25 位国家，按最高分期堆叠，条末为合计。**c**，六个主要国家按首次登记年份的累积试验数（2026 年截至 10 月 20 日，不完整）。**d**，前 15 位国家按试验与 *RAS* 的关系分类：RAS-targeted，药物直接作用于 RAS；RAS-regulator-targeted，作用于 SOS1、SHP2 或法尼基化；RAS-biomarker-selected，*RAS* 基因型决定入排或分层；RAS-mentioned，仅提及。数据仅取自 ClinicalTrials.gov（REST API v2，截取日 2026-10-20）；仅在 ChiCTR 或中国药监登记者（如 sosimerasib 单药Ⅱ期，ChiCTR2200059986）未纳入，中国占比偏低。
