## Figure 11 | Global distribution of RAS-related oncology trials registered on ClinicalTrials.gov

**a**, World map of the 1,133 interventional phase I–III oncology trials that
reference *RAS* as a target or as an eligibility biomarker, shaded by the number
of trials with at least one registered site in each country; countries with no
registered site are shown in the palest tint. Counts are trial–country pairs, so
a multinational trial contributes to every country in which it has a site
(1,054 trials with location data, 3,206 trial–country pairs, 25,966 site
records, 72 countries). Leader lines label the eleven busiest countries. Hong
Kong (37 trials) is merged into China for the cartography only; it is a separate
row in the underlying table. **b**, The 25 countries with the most trials,
stacked by the highest phase a trial reaches; totals are printed at the end of
each bar. **c**, Cumulative number of trials by the year the record was first
posted on ClinicalTrials.gov, for the six leading countries; 2026 is truncated
at the 20 October 2026 data cut and therefore underestimates that year. **d**,
Composition of the trial portfolio of the 15 leading countries by the way each
trial relates to *RAS*: RAS-targeted, the investigational agent acts on the RAS
protein itself; RAS-regulator-targeted, the agent acts on SOS1, SHP2 or protein
farnesylation; RAS-biomarker-selected, *RAS* genotype governs eligibility or
stratification while the agent is directed elsewhere; RAS-mentioned, *RAS* is
referred to but is neither target nor selection criterion. **Coverage limitation.** The dataset is drawn from ClinicalTrials.gov only. Studies registered
solely with the Chinese regulator or on ChiCTR — for example the phase 2 monotherapy study of
sosimerasib under ChiCTR2200059986 — are therefore absent, and the share of trials sponsored
from China is understated by this map. Data were harvested
from the ClinicalTrials.gov REST API v2 on 20 October 2026; curation rules,
exclusions and the full record-level table are given in the accompanying data
documentation.

# 中文对照

## 图11 ｜ ClinicalTrials.gov 登记的 RAS 相关恶性肿瘤临床试验的全球分布

**a**，1 133 项以 *RAS* 为靶点或入排生物标志物的干预性 I–III 期肿瘤试验的世界分布图，颜色深浅表示各国至少设有一个登记中心的试验数量，无登记中心的国家以最浅色示意。计数单位为"试验—国家对"，即一项多国研究在其设点的每个国家各计一次（有地点信息的试验 1 054 项，试验—国家对 3 206 个，中心记录 25 966 条，覆盖 72 个国家/地区）。引线标注试验数最多的 11 个国家。香港（37 项）**仅在制图时**并入中国，在数据表中仍为独立一行。**b**，试验数前 25 位国家，按该试验所达到的最高分期堆叠，条末数字为合计。**c**，六个主要国家按注册记录首次公开年份计算的累积试验数；2026 年截至 2026 年 10 月 20 日数据截取日，为不完整年份，必然低估。**d**，前 15 位国家的试验构成，按试验与 *RAS* 的关系划分：RAS-targeted 指在研药物直接作用于 RAS 蛋白；RAS-regulator-targeted 指作用于 SOS1、SHP2 或蛋白法尼基化等 RAS 调控/加工环节；RAS-biomarker-selected 指以 *RAS* 基因型决定入排或分层、但药物本身不针对 RAS；RAS-mentioned 指提及 *RAS* 但既非靶点也非入选条件。**覆盖范围的一处限制。**本文的试验登记数据集只取自 ClinicalTrials.gov：仅在中国药监或 ChiCTR 登记的研究不在其内，例如 sosimerasib 的单药Ⅱ期（ChiCTR2200059986），因此中国申办方研究的占比在这一口径下偏低。数据于 2026 年 10 月 20 日经 ClinicalTrials.gov REST API v2 抓取；纳排规则、排除清单与记录级总表见随附的数据说明文档。
