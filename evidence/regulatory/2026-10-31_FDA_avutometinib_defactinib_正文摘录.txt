【关键判定二：适应证原文（label，第 1 节 INDICATIONS AND USAGE）】
  "AVMAPKI FAKZYNJA CO-PACK is indicated for the treatment of adult patients with KRAS-mutated
   recurrent low-grade serous ovarian cancer (LGSOC) who have received prior systemic therapy."
  "This indication is approved under accelerated approval based on tumor response rate and duration of
   response [see Clinical Studies (14)]. Continued approval for this indication may be contingent upon
   verification and description of clinical benefit in a confirmatory trial."
即：KRAS 突变、复发性低级别浆液性卵巢癌、既往接受过系统治疗；以缓解率与缓解持续时间为据。
HIGHLIGHTS 首页同一句多一个同位语（"a combination of avutometinib and defactinib, each kinase
inhibitors"）；该页是双栏排版，pdftotext -layout 会把右栏并进同一物理行，逐字核引须只取
左栏片段（宽度 ≤84 字符）——2026-10-31 已按此核过两句都通过。
2.1 Patient Selection 另有一句（表2 附注引用）：
  "An FDA-approved test for the detection of a KRAS mutation in LGSOC for selecting patients for
   treatment with AVMAPKI FAKZYNJA CO-PACK is not available."
即本适应证**未附伴随诊断**，KRAS 突变由前瞻性本地检测（NGS 或 PCR）判定（第 14 节原文）。

【关键判定三：疗效人群 57 例，确认 ORR 44%（95% CI 31–58）——不是 37%】
label 第 14 节 CLINICAL STUDIES 逐字：
  "The efficacy of AVMAPKI FAKZYNJA CO-PACK was evaluated in RAMP-201 (NCT04625270), an
   open-label, multicenter study that included 57 adult patients with measurable
   KRAS-mutated recurrent LGSOC. Patients were required to have received at least one
   prior systemic therapy, including a platinum-based regimen."
  "The major efficacy outcome measure was overall response rate (ORR) assessed by blinded
   independent review committee (BIRC) according to Response Evaluation Criteria in Solid
   Tumors (RECIST), version 1.1."
  Table 5 Efficacy Results in RAMP-201（N = 57）：
    Confirmed Overall Response Rate (95% CI)  44% (31, 58)
      Complete response  3.5%；Partial response  40%
    Duration of Response (DoR) Range (months)  3.3, 31.1
  "The tumor KRAS mutations observed in the 25 responders were A146V, G12D, G12R, G12V,
   and Q61H."（25／57＝43.9%，与 44% 自洽）

【与督导 2026-10-30 清单的差异：ORR 37% 无出处】
`grep -n "37"` 对 label 全文只命中不良事件表的百分数（Dyspepsia 2、Dermatitis acneiform
37、Vitreoretinal disorders 37、Increased alkaline phosphatase 37），**CLINICAL STUDIES
一节没有 37% 这个缓解率**；批准函全文亦无 37%。上一轮清单把 RAMP 201 论文的
「115 例受治」与标签的「57 例」当成两套缓解率，实为三项分母：
  · 115 例：论文 `4.1_BanerjeeSN_2025` 受治人数（表 1 行首 "A total of 115 patients
    received the go-forward combination regimen"）；
  · 109 例：可评估人数，全人群确认 ORR 34/109＝31%（95% CI 23–41）；
  · 57 例：论文 TABLE 2 的 "KRAS mt, n = 57"，确认 ORR 25/57＝44%；**与 label 的
    57 例是同一群体**（KRAS 突变且可测病灶），故 label 与论文在这一格上一致。
据此本综述只写「57 例、确认 ORR 44%（95% CI 31–58，BIRC／RECIST 1.1）」，不写 37%。

【关键判定四：确证性研究 RAMP 301，标签与批准函都未点名，出处是论文】
label 与批准函只写 "a confirmatory trial(s)"，未给试验名。RAMP 301 的名字与登记号见
库内 `4.1_BanerjeeSN_2025` 原文：
  "randomized phase 3 study of avutometinib and defactinib versus investigator's ...
   (RAMP301; ClinicalTrials.gov identifier: NCT06072781)."
语料 `RAS临床试验注册分析/数据/表1_临床试验总表.csv` 的 NCT06072781 行：acronym
"RAMP 301"、Phase 3、RANDOMIZED、对照臂含 pegylated liposomal doxorubicin／paclitaxel／
letrozole／anastrozole（即研究者选择），RECRUITING。表2 的「确证性研究」一栏据此写
「RAMP 301（Ⅲ期）」，不作「标签点名」之误。
