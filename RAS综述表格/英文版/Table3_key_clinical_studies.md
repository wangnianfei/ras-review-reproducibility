Note: The table includes clinical studies cited in the text that report end-point results that can be restated; studies with a registration number but no readout are listed in the last group. Registration numbers are taken from the cited text or matched by study acronym in this review's trial registration dataset (ClinicalTrials.gov, cut-off 2026-10-20); SCARLET, NEMO and CCTG PA.7 were not found, and no number is presumed. "—" means that the original report did not give the item, and it was not retrieved from the original. Single-arm response rates are given with sample size and assessment method; negative results state direction, end point and comparator, and results not crossing the prespecified significance threshold are recorded as "did not reach prespecified significance". CodeBreaK 200 and KRYSTAL-12 permitted or experienced crossover and tested PFS, ORR and OS hierarchically, whereas RASolute 302 did not permit crossover; comparisons of OS across trials must separate differences in design from differences in efficacy. Approval information is in Table 2.

> Abbreviations: BICR, blinded independent central review; CNS, central nervous system; CRC / mCRC, (metastatic) colorectal cancer; DCR, disease control rate; DOR, duration of response; IRC / IRRC, independent (radiology) review committee; MTD, maximum tolerated dose; NSCLC, non-small-cell lung cancer; ORR, objective response rate; OS, overall survival; PDAC, pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma; PFS, progression-free survival; RP2D, recommended phase 2 dose; TRAE, treatment-related adverse event; VAF, variant allele frequency.
